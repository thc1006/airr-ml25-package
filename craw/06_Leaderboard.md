## Scoring Metric

The leaderboard score is a **weighted average** of:

1. **AUC-ROC (Area Under the ROC Curve)**: Measures classification performance for predicting label-positive repertoires

2. **Jaccard Similarity**: Measures the accuracy of identifying label-associated sequences

The final score combines both metrics across all datasets included in the competition.
